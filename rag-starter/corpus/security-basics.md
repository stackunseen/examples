# Security basics

## Passwords and sign-in

Use the company password manager for every work account. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, source control and the HR portal. Never reuse a work password anywhere else.

## Phishing

If an email asks for credentials, payment or urgent action, do not click its links. Report it with the Report Phishing button in your mail client, or forward it to security@example.com. Reporting a mistake quickly matters more than avoiding it.

## Lost or stolen devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT service desk within one hour, even outside working hours. The device can then be locked and wiped remotely. All company laptops have full-disk encryption switched on.

## Software installs

Install software only from the company software centre. Requests for anything else go through the IT portal and may need a security review.
