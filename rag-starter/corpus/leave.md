# Leave policy

## Annual leave

Full-time employees get 25 days of annual leave a year plus public holidays. Part-time employees get the same entitlement in proportion to their hours. Book leave in the HR portal at least two weeks ahead for anything longer than three days.

## Carrying leave over

You can carry up to five unused days into the next year. Carried days must be used by the end of March, after which they are lost. Exceptions need written approval from HR.

## Parental leave

The primary carer gets sixteen weeks of parental leave on full pay, followed by up to twenty-six weeks on statutory pay. The secondary carer gets four weeks on full pay, which can be taken in two blocks within the first year.

## Sick leave

If you are unwell, tell your manager before your usual start time. From the eighth day of absence you need a note from a doctor. Sick days do not reduce your annual leave.
