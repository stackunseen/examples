# Procurement

## Buying something

Purchases under 5,000 pounds need your budget holder's approval in the purchasing system. Purchases of 5,000 pounds or more need two written quotes. Anything over 50,000 pounds goes to the procurement team for a tender.

## New software and vendors

Any new software that will hold company or customer data needs a security review before purchase. Vendors that process personal data also need a signed data processing agreement.

## Signing contracts

Only directors may sign contracts. Contracts longer than two years, or with automatic renewal, also need review by the legal team.
