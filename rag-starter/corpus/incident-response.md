# Incident response

## Severity levels

Sev1 means customers cannot use a core product or data may be exposed. Sev2 means a feature is degraded with a workaround. Sev3 covers minor issues that can wait for working hours.

## Paging and acknowledgement

A Sev1 pages the on-call engineer and the incident commander at once. The on-call engineer acknowledges within fifteen minutes, day or night. Sev2 is acknowledged within one hour during working hours.

## Communication

The incident commander posts an update to the status channel every thirty minutes during a Sev1. Customer-facing messages are written by the support lead, not by engineers.

## Postmortems

Every Sev1 and Sev2 gets a written postmortem within five working days. Postmortems are blameless and end with owned actions and due dates.
