# Remote work

## Working from home

You can work from home up to three days a week, agreed with your manager. Core hours are ten until four, when you should be reachable.

## Working from abroad

You may work from another country for up to twenty working days a year. Tell HR at least two weeks before you go, because tax and insurance rules depend on the country.

## Home office equipment

New starters get a one-off home office allowance of 300 pounds for a chair, a desk or a monitor. Claim it through the expenses system within your first three months.
