# Travel and expenses policy

## Who this covers

This policy applies to all employees and contractors travelling on company business. Your manager approves a trip before anything is booked. Finance approves anything above the limits below.

## Booking travel

Book through the company travel desk at least fourteen days ahead where you can. Economy class is the default for flights under six hours. Premium economy is allowed for flights over six hours with your manager's approval. Rail is preferred over flying for journeys under four hours.

## Hotels

Hotels are reimbursed up to 180 pounds per night in London and 140 pounds per night elsewhere in the United Kingdom. Outside the United Kingdom the limit is the local rate that finance publishes each January. Breakfast is included in the nightly limit when the hotel charges it separately.

## Meals

Meals are reimbursed up to 45 pounds per day when you travel overnight and up to 20 pounds for a day trip. Alcohol is not reimbursed. Client dinners need a named client and are approved by finance, not by your manager.

## Claims

Submit a claim within thirty days of returning, with itemised receipts. Claims older than ninety days are not paid. Card statements are not accepted as receipts.
