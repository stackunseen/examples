# IT and access

## Your first day

Your laptop is ready on day one if your manager orders it at least five working days before you start. You sign in with your company account and enrol your phone for multi-factor authentication during setup.

## Requesting access

Request access to systems and shared drives through the IT portal. Your manager approves the request, and the owner of the system approves anything Confidential or Restricted. Access is reviewed every six months and removed if unused.

## Remote access

There is no VPN. Company apps check your identity and your device's health on every sign-in, so they work the same from home, the office or a hotel.

## Leaving the company

Your accounts are disabled at the end of your last working day. Return your laptop and badge to IT, or use the prepaid courier box that IT sends you.
