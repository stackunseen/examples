# Data handling

## Classification levels

Every document and dataset has one of four labels: Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Restricted covers customer personal data, payroll, health information and security keys. When in doubt, use the higher label.

## Using AI tools

Public and Internal data may be used with the approved AI assistant. Confidential data may be used only in the approved assistant, never in external chatbots. Restricted data must not be pasted into any AI tool, approved or not.

## Sharing outside the company

Confidential documents can be shared outside the company only through the secure file portal, with an expiry date. Restricted data is never shared externally without a signed data processing agreement.

## Retention

Keep customer records for six years after the contract ends, then delete them. Chat transcripts from the AI assistant are kept for ninety days.
